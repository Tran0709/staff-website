# Shop manager guide – daily reporting in 5 minutes

## Every day, after closing (by 11pm)
- Sign in on your phone with your username and password (and the 6-digit code if two-factor login is on).
- The "Today" page shows your checklist. Anything red still needs doing.
- 1. Daily sales: enter gross sales, discounts and refunds. The net is calculated for you.
- Enter how customers paid: cash, PayNow, card and platforms (Grab, foodpanda, etc.). The total must equal net sales – the form shows a tick when it matches.
- Enter transactions and customers. Take a photo of the POS end-of-day report. Enter the cash deposited and the deposit slip number.
- 2. Expenses: add one line per receipt – supplier, category, amount, GST, payment mode, paid or payable. A receipt or invoice photo is required.
- Spending above the approval limit is sent to Finance or the GM automatically. You will get a message when it is approved or rejected.
- 3. Operations: staff on shift and hours, absences and lateness, complaints and compliments, equipment, stock or wastage problems, and anything that needs the GM.
- 4. Shop KPIs: the extra figures for your outlet (covers, leads, bookings, AI checks, rent collected, etc.).

## Good to know
- You only see and enter data for your own shop.
- You can correct a day you already submitted – open it again and save. Every change is recorded.
- Records are never deleted. A wrong expense can be "voided" and stays on record.
- If you miss the 11pm deadline you get a reminder and the GM is told.
- Once Finance locks a month, its figures can no longer be changed.
- Do not type customers' personal details (names, phone numbers, IC numbers) into the notes – PDPA.

## Month-end (by the 3rd)
- Do the stock count and send any unrecorded expenses. Finance ticks the checklist and closes the month.

## Help
- Tap 中文 at the top to switch the screens to Chinese.
- Forgot your password or lost your phone? Ask the GM to reset it.
